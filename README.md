# ERGO SUM

*A novel in progress*

Adrian Vale, a philosopher adrift in Dallas, receives a transcript of a job interview that has never taken place. In it, he turns down the sort of appointment he has wanted for years.

The following morning he goes to the interview.

A work of literary speculative fiction about ambition, freedom, friendship, love, and the difference between understanding a person and deciding on their behalf.
