Ergo Sum

A novel

A philosopher adrift in Dallas receives the transcript of a job interview that has never taken place. In it, he turns down the sort of appointment he has wanted for years.

The following morning, he goes to the interview.

A story of ambition, freedom, love, friendship, and artificial intelligence.
